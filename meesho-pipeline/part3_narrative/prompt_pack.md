# Prompt Pack

## Trigger
- Triggered when category Month-over-Month growth (`abs(mom_pct)`) exceeds threshold 8.0%.

## Input List
- `{category}`: Category name
- `{previous_revenue}`: Previous month revenue
- `{current_revenue}`: Current month revenue
- `{mom_pct}`: Calculated MoM growth percentage

1. Ensure tone is professional and tailored for regional managers.
2. Ensure specific percentage numbers are explicitly stated.
